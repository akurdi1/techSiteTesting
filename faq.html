FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to common questions about Techcamp websites, apps, software, hosting and support.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages, content, integrations and design requirements. A simple business website can be much faster than a custom platform.

Can Techcamp build Android and iOS applications?

Yes. We can design mobile application experiences and structure projects for Android, iOS or cross-platform delivery.

Can you build custom POS software?

Yes. POS and business management systems are part of Techcamp's custom software offering.

Do you provide hosting and domains?

Yes. Techcamp offers domain and hosting packages with technical support. Current package details should be confirmed before purchase.

Can you add Kurdish and Arabic?

Yes. Websites and applications can be designed for multilingual content, including RTL layouts for Arabic and Kurdish content.

Can you connect a frontend to my existing backend?

Yes. The frontend can be connected later to an API, PostgreSQL, Supabase or another backend depending on the project architecture.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, fixes, updates, deployment and technical support can be included in the project or handled as an ongoing service.
